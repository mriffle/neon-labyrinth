Privacy Policy

Effective August 2, 2026

Neon Labyrinth is a screensaver for Roku devices. It is designed to run entirely on your device, and its approach to your data is simple: it doesn't want any.

What Neon Labyrinth does — and doesn't do

  • No account. The screensaver does not create, require, or connect to any account.
  • No data collection. It does not collect, store, or transmit personal information of any kind.
  • No advertising or analytics. It contains no ads, no third-party analytics, and no tracking technology.
  • No network use. It runs offline. It makes no network requests.
  • Settings stay local. Your preferences (palette, complexity, speed, and similar options) are stored only on your Roku device, using Roku's standard local settings storage. They never leave the device.
  • Purchases are handled by Roku. Buying Neon Labyrinth happens through the Roku Channel Store under Roku's own policies. We never see your payment details. Roku's handling of your purchase is described in Roku's privacy policy.

This website

This site is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and includes no analytics or third-party scripts or fonts. GitHub may log standard technical request data (such as IP addresses) as part of hosting; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If a future version of Neon Labyrinth ever changes any of the above, this policy will be updated first, with a new effective date, before that version is released.
